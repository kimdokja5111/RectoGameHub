INDEPENDENT BY DESIGN

A home for the feeling.

RetroGameHub is a small discovery guide for games that bring a little classic-era magic into the present.

✳

Why we’re here

Some games bring back the sense of getting lost in a new world: chunky shapes, bold ideas, odd corners, and a soundtrack that stays with you. RetroGameHub collects modern indie games, retro-style adventures, and a few open projects that carry that spirit forward.

How the guide works

Browse by genre, platform, year, or feel. Our guide scores and discovery values are editorial browsing aids, not official reviews, sales figures, or claims about a game’s standing in the wider market.

Games stay with their makers

RetroGameHub does not host, mirror, or distribute game files. Commercial titles link to legitimate store pages; free and open-source projects link to official project pages. Game titles and trademarks belong to their respective owners. RetroGameHub is independent and is not affiliated with Sony, PlayStation, Microsoft, Nintendo, or any publisher or developer listed here.
